# Chinook Music Store Analysis

**Question:** Who are the store's customers, what did they buy, and which sales agents brought in the most revenue?

**Data:** The Chinook sample database: customers, invoices, invoice lines, tracks, albums, artists and employees.

**SQL used:**

- Multi-table joins across up to four tables
- Filtering by country, title and date range
- Aggregations with `GROUP BY`, `ROUND` and `ORDER BY ... LIMIT` to rank agents
