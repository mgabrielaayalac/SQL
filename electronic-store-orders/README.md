# Electronic Store Customer & Order Analytics

**Question:** How many orders did the store take each month, which products drove revenue, and how much do customers who buy several items at once spend?

**Data:** Monthly sales tables (January, February) and a customers table from an electronics retailer.

**SQL used:**

- Joins between customer and sales tables
- Aggregations: `COUNT`, `SUM`, `AVG`
- Subqueries
- Data-quality filters to exclude malformed order IDs and header rows
